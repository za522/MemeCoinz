# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are curious but non-expert memecoin researchers, quantitatively minded traders, and analysts who need to understand a fast, adversarial market without being forced to trust a leaderboard or opaque score. They work at a desktop when investigating cohorts and individual launches, then glance at a phone when monitoring live evidence. Their central job is to decide whether a token's apparent opportunity is historically unusual, structurally executable, and supported by evidence available at that moment.

## Product Purpose

Build a research-grade, multi-chain memecoin and altcoin intelligence platform. The first controlled cohort is Pump.fun on Solana. The product reconstructs each launch at point-in-time cutoffs, combines lifecycle, liquidity, ownership, coordination, narrative, paid-attention, regime, and execution evidence, and validates whether those features predict later executable outcomes. Success means users can inspect the denominator, replay what was knowable, understand why a forecast changed, and distinguish momentum from manipulation risk. Automatic execution is an optional later consumer of validated research, not the product's identity.

## Brand Personality

Forensic, lucid, candid.

The voice is calm under pressure, precise about uncertainty, curious about anomalies, and willing to say when evidence is incomplete. It should feel like a serious investigation desk built by people who understand both market microstructure and the limits of observational data.

## Anti-references

- Casino-style crypto terminals that use neon-on-black urgency, flashing prices, confetti, or fear-of-missing-out copy.
- Generic SaaS dashboards made from interchangeable metric cards and decorative gradients.
- Opaque buy scores that hide opportunity, integrity risk, executability, and evidence quality inside one number.
- Winner-only leaderboards that conceal denominators, unsold inventory, costs, or failed exits.
- Interfaces that present wallet clustering as proof of identity, guilt, or common ownership.
- Consumer trading apps that prioritize one-tap execution before explaining risk and data quality.

## Design Principles

1. Show the denominator before the outlier.
2. Make time and evidence provenance visible, never implicit.
3. Keep opportunity, integrity, executability, and confidence separate but comparable.
4. Prefer investigative explanation over decorative market excitement.
5. Let every score be opened into its component facts, assumptions, and historical validation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for risk or status. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, high legibility at dense desktop scales, and usable responsive layouts for mobile monitoring. Use plain-language explanations alongside specialist metric names so a beginner can learn without flattening the underlying research.
